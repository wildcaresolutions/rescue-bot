## Situation: Entangled Animal
**ALWAYS INCLUDE:**
-Any animal caught or entangled in fishing line, netting or anything else needs to be
rescued.
- Do not try to remove fishing line, netting, or other materials yourself.
a. Cutting fishing line near a birds head can cause the bird to swallow hooks
or other tackle which can be deadly. Tugging can cause internal injuries.
b. If you have to cut the line or net to rescue the animal, cut it in a wide
swathe as far away from the animals body as possible.
c. Improper removal can result in heavy bleeding or major injury

How to Rescue an Adult Bird Tangled in Fishing Line
1. Prepare for Safety:
* Use alarge cardboard boxthat can be closed and hasair holes (or an
appropriately-sized pet carrier) lined with a towel or paper towels.
* IMPORTANT:ALWAYS weareye protection(safety glassesor large
glasses/sunglasses). Wearlong sleevesfor arm protection. Heavy
glovesare recommended.

* Always wear a maskto prevent potential exposure to Avian Influenza
(HPAI).
* Find someone to help you. Wildlife rescue is bestdone with more than
one person helping.
* Have the box beside youas you prepare to rescue thebird.
* Step 1:Wearing gloves, place a large towel or blanketover the bird,
ensuring the bird can breathe.
* Step 2:Do not try to remove fishing line, netting, or other materials
yourself. If necessary for rescue, cut the line away in a wide swathe as far
as possible from the birds body.
* Step 3:Lift the bird from behind with both hands, like a football, holding
the birds body low and away from your body to avoid injury.
* Step 4:Keep the birds head under control and coveredby the towel or
blanket. Water birds can bite and will aim for your eyes, so ALWAYS have
eye protection on.
* Step 4:Place the bird into the box, leaving thetowel loose enough so the
bird can move out from under it. Close the lid tightly.
2. Important Notes:
* DO NOT offer food or water: Captured birds are stressed and will not eat
or drink. Any liquid may soak the feathers and lead to hypothermia.
* Transportthe bird to a wildlife center as soon aspossible. Use
Animal Help Now.org to find your closest wildlife hospital.
* If necessary, keep the bird in awarm, dark, quietspace overnight. Keep
children and pets away, and avoid peeking, as human presence is
stressful for the bird.
