DOCUMENT: GULL (SEAGULL) RESCUE AND CARE
Important Safety Reminder:
* Gulls can be carriers of Avian Influenza (HPAI). Always wear eye protection,
gloves, and a maskwhen handling these birds. Washh and s and shoes with
soap and waterafter handling these birds to reducethe risk of transmission.
Gull / Seagull - Rescue Information:
* Anyadult gullthat does not fly or walk away whenapproached is likely in
distress and needs immediate rescue.
* Anybaby gullfound on the ground or alone shouldbe rescued immediately. The
baby will need to be kept warm.

Section 1: How to Rescue an Adult Gull / Seagull
1. Prepare for Safety:
* Use alarge cardboard boxthat can be closed and hasair holes (or an
appropriately-sized pet carrier) lined with a towel or paper towels.
* IMPORTANT:ALWAYS weareye protection(safety glassesor large
glasses/sunglasses). Wearlong sleevesfor arm protection. Heavy
glovesare recommended.
* Always wear a maskto prevent potential exposure to Avian Influenza
(HPAI).
* Have the box beside youas you prepare to rescue thebird.
* Find someone to help you. Wildlife rescue is bestdone with more than
one person helping.
i. Step 1:Wearing gloves, place a large towel or blanketover the
bird, ensuring the bird can breathe.
ii. Step 2:Lift the bird from behind with both hands, like a football,
holding the birds body low and away from your body to avoid injury.

iii. Step 3:Keep the birds head under control and covered by the
towel or blanket. These birds can bite and will aim for your eyes, so
ALWAYS have eye protection on.
iv. Step 4:Place the bird into the box, leaving thetowel loose enough
so the bird can move out from under it. Close the lid tightly.
2. Important Notes:
* DO NOT offer food or water: Captured birds are stressed and will not eat
or drink. Any liquid may soak the feathers and lead to hypothermia.
* Transportthe bird to a wildlife center as soon aspossible.
* If necessary, keep the bird in awarm, dark, quietspace overnight. Keep
children and pets away, and avoid peeking, as human presence is
stressful for the bird.

Section 2: How to Rescue a Baby Gull / Seagull
1. Any gull on the ground is in trouble and in need of rescue.
2. Get Help:Ideally, recruit anot her person to helpyou capture the young bird.
3. A baby gull that is covered in downy fluff and does not yet have adult feathers
needs immediate care and warmth.
4. Prepare
* Get along-hand led netand aboxlined with a towel or paper towels. It
should have a secure lid and air holes.
* If you don't have a net, you can gently cover the bird with alight towel or
T-shirt, but if the bird is in the water, be sureto lift him out of the water
immediately to prevent drowning.
* Wear light glovesor use a tea towel to cover yourh and s to protect
against potential bites or scratches and to avoid parasites like feather lice.
* Always wear eye protection as even baby herons and egrets have sharp
beaks

5. Rescue Procedure:
* Step 1:Approach carefully and toss the light towel or T-shirt over him.
* Step 2:Gentlyscoop the birdinto the box using thenet or towel.
* Step 3:Make sure the bird'shead is uncoveredinthe box to allow for
breathing.
* Step 4:If its wet, gently shake loose the materialor net and remove it
from the box to prevent the bird from getting cold.
* Step 5:Close the box securely, and bring the birdto Wild Care or your
local wildlife care center as soon as possible.
6. Important Notes:
* If there is a delay in getting the bird to expert help, place the box with the
baby bird in it in awarm, dark, quiet place, likea closet or bathroom.
The baby bird MUST be kept warm. Ask for more details on keeping him
warm.
* Keep children and pets awayto avoid causing furtherstress.
* DO NOT feedthe baby bird. Feeding should only bedone under
professional guidance.

Final Reminders:
* Always prioritize safety: If unsure about handling, seek trained wildlife

themselves
* HPAI Protection:Always useeye protection, gloves, and a maskwhen
