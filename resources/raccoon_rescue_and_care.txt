RACCOON RESCUE AND CARE
IMPORTANT: Raccoons are rabies vector species, and handling adult raccoons can be
dangerous. It is essential to seek professional help when rescuing an adult raccoon. If
you are bitten or scratched, seek immediate medical attention.
RESCUE STEP 1. Determine the age of the raccoon.
Observe the raccoon's size and physical features to determine her age.
1. Neonate (Newborn)
* Appearance:Pink and naked, eyes sealed shut.
* Care Needed:Immediate warmth and care.
2. Juvenile
* Appearance:Small, wobbly, eyes open or closed, shortsoft fur, thin short
tail.
* Care Needed:Immediate warmth and care.
3. Adult or Subadult
* Appearance:Large, fully furred, fluffy striped tail.
* Note:Adult raccoons can bedangerousto handle.
RESCUE STEP 2: Determine if the animal requires rescue
* A neonate raccoon ALWAYS needs rescue.
* A juvenile raccoon ALWAYS needs rescue
* An adult raccoon that cannot or does not try to get away from you likely needs
rescue.
RESCUE STEP 3: Determine if wildlife professionals should be called in
* Neonate raccoon: No. You should be able to safely rescue this baby raccoon
yourself
* Juvenile raccoon: No. With proper protective gear, you should be able to rescue
this young raccoon yourself.
* Adult raccoon: Yes. Adult raccoons are rabies vector species and can be very
hazardous to handle. Always call for professional assistance before attempting to
rescue an adult raccoon.
**IF animal age is adult ALWAYS INCLUDE: Rescuing an adult raccoon can be very
hazardous. If you cannot reach professional assistance, and if you feel safe doing so,
please follow the instructions below to safely rescue the adult raccoon.

**IF animal age is neonate: ALWAYS INCLUDE: You should be able to safely rescue
this baby raccoon. Please follow the instructions below.
**IF animal age is juvenile: ALWAYS INCLUDE: With proper protective gear, you
should be able to safely rescue this baby raccoon. Please follow the instructions below.
RESCUE STEP 4: Collect protective gear as needed
**IF animal age is adult ALWAYS INCLUDE: Heavy gloves, long sleeves, eye
protection, two heavy towels or blankets. Note that gardening gloves, ski gloves or
mechanics gloves are too thin and will not offer enough protection. Get heavy gloves
and a thick towel or blanket for protection. The raccoons teeth can easily puncture light
gloves and cause serious injury.
**IF animal age is juvenile: ALWAYS INCLUDE: Gloves, long sleeves, eye protection,
two towels or blankets
**IF animal age is neonate: ALWAYS INCLUDE: Light gloves or lightweight towel
RESCUE STEP 5: Prepare a container for the rescued animal
**IF animal age is adult ALWAYS INCLUDE: Use a large box or bin with a lid and
pre-punched air holes or a large pet carrier. Line the container with a towel.
**IF animal age is juvenile: ALWAYS INCLUDE: Use a box or bin with a lid and
pre-punched air holes or a pet carrier. Line the container with a towel.
**IF animal age is neonate: ALWAYS INCLUDE: Use a small box or bin with a lid and
pre-punched air holes or a small pet carrier. Line the container with a lightweight towel.
You will need to provide heat for this baby raccoon.
RESCUE STEP 6: Find an assistant. Wildlife rescueis easier with more than one
person helping.
RESCUE STEP 7: With the container beside you, throw something over the animal
to restrain him.
**IF animal age is adult: Toss a towel/blanket over the raccoon to restrain him, making
note of where his head is.
**IF animal age is juvenile: Gently place a towel over the young raccoon to restrain
him, making note of where his head is.
**IF animal age is neonate: Gently place a towel over the baby raccoon.

RESCUE STEP 8: With hands protected, lift the animal and place him in the box.
Close the box.
**IF animal age is adult ALWAYS INCLUDE: Wearing the heavy gloves, or using a
second towel to protect your hands, lift the raccoon from behind, holding his head away
from your body. IMPORTANT: Do not put yourself at risk.
**IF animal age is juvenile: Gently lift the young raccoon in the towel with both hands
and place him in the container. Do not wrap him tightly, make sure he can move out
from under the towel.
**IF animal age is neonate: Gently lift the young raccoon in the towel with both hands
and place him in the container.
RESCUE STEP 9: Does the animal need to be kept warm? Follow instructions if
so.
**IF animal age is adult: No. An adult raccoon does not need supplemental head
**IF animal age is juvenile: Yes. A juvenile raccoon should have a supplemental heat
source if you cant get him to care immediately. IMPORTANT: Only place the heat
source under half of the container so the animal has the option to roll off the heat if he
gets too hot.
**IF animal age is neonate: Yes. A newborn mammal of any species needs
supplemental heat. IMPORTANT: Only place the heat source under half of the container
so the animal has the option to roll off the heat if he gets too hot.
RESCUE STEP 10: Transport the animal to care as soon as possible.
ALWAYS INCLUDE: Do NOT offer food or water. A stressedanimal will not eat, and
food and water will spill and stick to the birds feathers. Stay quiet and do not peek at
the animal because human presence is stressful for wildlife.
