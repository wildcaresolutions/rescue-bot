Wild Care Operating Hours
* Normal Operating Hours:
Open daily, 9 AM PST to 5 PM PST.
Call415-456-7283for assistance with wildlife.
* Emergency Nightline Hours:
Open daily, 5 PM PST to 9 PM PST.
Call415-456-7283for wildlife emergencies.
* For Wildlife Assistance Outside Marin County or the San Francisco Bay Area:
Visitahnow.orgto find your local wildlife hospital.

What to Do Outside of Operating Hours
If neither Wild Care nor the Emergency Nightline is open, follow these steps:
* For Marin County residents:
Call Marin Humane:(415) 883-4621and press 1 for help.
* For others:
Visitahnow.orgto find your nearest wildlife hospital.
* For Animal Control Assistance:
Animal control should be a secondary option if Wild Care or anot her wildlife hospital are
unavailable. If directed by Wild Care or your local hospital, you may contact animal
control for handling adult animals or rabies-vector species.
* For Marin County Animal Control:
Call(415) 883-4621and press "1".
* For other areas:
Search for your local animal controlhere.

Wild Cares Address and Directions
Wild Care is located at our Interim location off North San Pedro Road in San Rafael, Marin
County.
Wild Care Interim is located at 37 Schmidt Lane, behind 251 North San Pedro Road. Our Wildlife
Hospital is open to admit injured and orphaned wildlife patients from 9 AM to 5 PM, seven days
a week.
Driving Directions:
Take the North San Pedro Road exit (454 northbound, 454B southbound) from Highway 101.
Follow North San Pedro Road east for 0.7 miles (past the Osher Marin JCC).

Turn left on Schmidt Lane.
Continue straight at the stop sign at Chalda Court.
At 37 Schmidt Lane, turn left into the driveway for the baseball field and Wild Care Interim.
Wild Care Interim is at the top of the paved driveway.
