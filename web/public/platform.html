The Network

A coalition, not a SaaS.

WildCare Solutions is a coalition of California wildlife rehabilitation organizations sharing one thing: the knowledge it takes to answer the phone correctly when someone calls about an injured animal at 11pm.

Each member brings their own expertise to the network. The AI assistant is the delivery mechanism. The protocols, the field experience, the species knowledge — all of that comes from people who handle real wildlife emergencies every day.

Wildlife Rescue Assistant
there's a bat flying around my living room what do i do
Stay calm — here's what to do RIGHT NOW:

1. Open all windows and exterior doors in the room
2. Turn off lights inside, turn on outside lights
3. Leave the room and close interior doors so the bat stays in one area
4. Most bats fly out on their own within 15–30 min

IMPORTANT: Do NOT touch the bat with bare hands. Bats can carry rabies.

Did anyone in your household have direct contact with the bat (touched it, woke up with it in the room)?
no nobody touched it. it just got in through a window
Good. No exposure means no rabies risk for the people. Let the bat fly out on its own. If it doesn't leave within 30 min, I can walk you through safe capture (gloves + a container). Want that?

How to Join

Three steps. About a week.

1

Apply

Tell us about your org in one sentence. About a minute. WildCare's team reviews each application individually.

2

Onboarding call

30-min call to configure your branded widget — your colors, your phone number, your local protocols. Same week you apply.

3

Embed and go live

Paste one script tag on your website. Your branded bot is answering wildlife calls 24/7. Free for community members.
